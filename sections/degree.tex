\begin{definition}[Final Degree Model]
	In the Final Degree Model, The algoritm has, at all times, the knowledge of the what the degree of all offline edges is in the final graph. This allows an algorithm to know how many opportunities there will be for a vertex to be matched and let it focus on the most urgent vertex. 
\end{definition}

A natural expression of this idea is the effective degree algorithm.
